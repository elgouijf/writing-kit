# O. Paragraphs, structure and synthesis

- **O1. Be synthetic, but complete enough to be understood.** The text does not cover everything that was done; "synthetic" does not mean "few lines". (school)
- **O2. One subject per paragraph.** A sentence goes in the paragraph about its subject, or gets its own paragraph with a topic sentence. (user)
- **O3. A short paragraph is not a weak one.** Judge a paragraph by what it says, never by its length. (user)
- **O4. A paragraph explains an idea; it is never built out of examples.** At most one example per point, woven into a sentence, and only when it clarifies. Never a point followed by sentences walking through examples (profiles P3 and P6 set their own example policy). (user)
- **O5. Synthesise instead of enumerating.** Drop the second and third illustration of the same point and add the general idea that ties them together. (user)
- **O6. No repetition.** Scan repeated 5-grams across sections before delivering, including between the abstract, the introduction and the conclusion. (user)
- **O7. Merge two points that are one, drop one that is not a point**, and update in the same edit any count that announces them. (user)
- **O8. Split a long list by position ("the first four…, the last four…"), never by an invented theme.** (user)
- **O9. No forced common thesis across unrelated topics, and no remark that they have nothing in common.** Just stop discussing it. (user)
- **O10. State the personal contribution once, unambiguously, in one place.** An attribution table or one paragraph; no personal-contribution section repeated under every project. (school, user)
- **O11. The conclusion is a broad synthesis with no numbers.** Broad is not vague: name the objects. (user)
- **O12. A critical section keeps only real limitations.** A correct design decision is not a limitation, even in a review; write it as a decision with its reason. Drop self-critique that serves no reader. (user)
- **O13. Under a page budget, pay for added prose with prose removed**, and check the length after every change (in a report, the page where "References" starts). (school)
- **O14. Heavy mechanism and new measured material go to the appendix**, and the body keeps the verdict. Pack appendices to limit blank space; take their letters from `\ref`, never typed by hand. (user)
- **O15. Frame objectives the way the requester sees them.** (review)
- **O16. Follow a required outline when one exists, and keep every assessed point.** A school outline may be adapted, but none of its assessed points disappears; use its words for the section titles it names. (school)
- **O17. Not a chronological account.** Gather the material, rank it, keep the most relevant, and restructure it into a few pages. (school)
